THE ASSIGNMENT

This was a game story I wrote during my internship with the Rockford Rivets. I wanted to make this story exciting and weave in some references to the movie "A League of Their Own" because Peaches Night was to honor the Rockford Peaches, who inspired the film. I completed an interview with the coach postgame and wrote the story and posted it that night.

“I challenged myself to write in a creative yet objective way, where I did not use the same exact formula for all of my game stories that I wrote for the Rivets.”
